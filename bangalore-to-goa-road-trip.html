DAY 2

Exploring Goa on a Scooty – Dolphins, Forts & Temples

The next morning we decided to experience Goa like locals — by renting a scooty. Two wheels gave us complete freedom to explore at our own pace.

🌊 Highlight of the Trip

Dolphin Point – A Magical First-Time Experience

Watching dolphins appear and disappear in the Arabian Sea was an incredible experience. Moments like these become lifelong memories because photographs and videos can never fully capture the excitement of seeing wildlife naturally in its environment.

Aguada Fort – Exploring History

This historical Portuguese fort offers amazing views and rich history. Walking around the fort felt like stepping into Goa's colonial past.

Portuguese Architecture Sea Views Photography Spot

The Famous Love Mansion

A famous mansion reportedly built by a diamond merchant for the love of his life. The architecture, design, and history behind this place make it truly unique and romantic.

Hidden Gem Romantic History Unique Architecture

Aguada Central Jail Museum

The old Aguada Central Jail has been converted into a museum preserving Goa's history. The transformation from an old prison into a heritage site creates an interesting and thought-provoking visitor experience.

Heritage Museum Historical Near Aguada Fort

DAY 3

Churches, Beaches & Goa Nightlife

The third day focused on exploring Goa's famous churches, iconic beaches, vibrant nightlife, and ending with a peaceful private villa at Morjim.

Basilica of Bom Jesus

One of the most iconic churches in Goa and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The stunning baroque architecture and historical significance make it a must-visit place on any Goa trip.

Se Cathedral Church

Another major attraction, Se Cathedral is one of the largest churches in Asia. The beautiful structure and peaceful atmosphere make it one of Goa's most visited places.

🏖️ The Three Beaches of Goa – Three Different Vibes

Calangute Beach – The Sunset Experience

Sunset

Known as the "Queen of Beaches," Calangute offered us the perfect sunset experience with beach activities, crowds enjoying the evening, and relaxing ocean vibes.

🌅 Beautiful Sunsets 🏄 Beach Activities 🍽️ Beach Shacks

Baga Beach – Goa's Party Capital

🔥 Nightlife

This place had a completely different energy. Among all the beaches we visited, the party vibe at Baga Beach was something we truly enjoyed. Music, restaurants, and vibrant beach culture make it unmissable.

🎵 Party Atmosphere 🕺 Nightlife 🍹 Beach Clubs 🎶 Live Music

Morjim Beach – Peaceful Private Paradise

🌿 Serene

After experiencing the energy of Baga, Morjim felt completely different. Instead of crowds and loud music, we found peaceful surroundings, cleaner atmosphere, and a private beach feel. We checked into Goa Beach Resort, where we got a beautiful private villa.

🏡 Private Villa ☮️ Peaceful 🦢 Turtle Beach 🧘 Relaxing

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Bangalore to Goa road trip.

The best route is Bangalore → Chitradurga → Hubli → Alnavar → Goa, covering approximately 700 KM in about 12 hours. This route offers smooth four-lane highways for most of the journey, scenic countryside views, multiple food stops, and a beautiful ghat section as you enter Goa. It's the most popular and comfortable route for families.

The drive from Bangalore to Goa takes approximately 12 hours of pure driving covering around 700 KM via the Chitradurga-Hubli route. Including breaks for breakfast, lunch, and rest, plan for 13-14 hours total. Starting at 5-6 AM from Bangalore will get you to Goa by evening, just in time for sunset.

Top places to visit in Goa in 2026 include: Dolphin Point (wild dolphin spotting), Aguada Fort (Portuguese history), Basilica of Bom Jesus (UNESCO World Heritage Site), Se Cathedral, Mahalasa Temple, Mangeshi Temple, Calangute Beach (sunsets), Baga Beach (nightlife), and Morjim Beach (peaceful private vibes). Hidden gems include the famous love mansion and Aguada Central Jail Museum.

Yes, the Bangalore to Goa road trip is completely safe for families. The highways are well-maintained with four-lane roads for most of the route. There are plenty of food stops, fuel stations, and rest areas along the way. Goa itself is very family-friendly with options ranging from luxury resorts to peaceful beaches. Just ensure you start early, take regular breaks, and drive carefully through the ghat section near Goa.

Ocean Beach Resort is an excellent luxury option with premium jacuzzi rooms and beautiful ambience. For a more private experience, Goa Beach Resort at Morjim offers private villas with direct beach access. Both properties exceeded our expectations and are perfect for family vacations.

The best time for a Bangalore to Goa road trip is from October to March when the weather is pleasant and ideal for beach activities. Long weekends in May are also popular despite the heat, as schools have holidays. Avoid the monsoon season (June-September) as the ghat roads can be slippery and beach activities are limited.

Yes! Dolphin Point in Goa is a popular spot where you can see wild dolphins in their natural habitat. Early morning boat trips offer the best chances of spotting dolphins in the Arabian Sea.
