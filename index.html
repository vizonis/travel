Where to Stay — Luxury & Budget Options

We don't just show you the destination — we help you plan the entire trip. Here are handpicked stay options at every budget for the most popular road trip destinations from Bangalore. Prices are approximate and vary by season.

Coorg (Kodagu)

Luxury Stay

Taj Madikeri Resort & Spa

5-star luxury nestled in 180 acres of rainforest. Infinity pool, spa, coffee plantation walks, and world-class dining. The kind of place you go to completely disconnect.

₹12,000 – ₹25,000/night

Best for: Honeymoons, luxury weekends, complete relaxation

Budget Stay

Coorg Cottage Homestays

Authentic Coorg-style homestays with home-cooked Pandi curry, coffee estate views, and warm hospitality. Clean rooms, local experience, fraction of resort prices.

₹1,500 – ₹4,000/night

Best for: Backpackers, solo travelers, authentic local experience

Goa

Luxury Stay

W Goa (Vagator)

Cliff-top luxury with Arabian Sea views. Private beach access, iconic W bar, infinity pool, and DJ nights. The ultimate Goa party-luxury experience.

₹18,000 – ₹45,000/night

Best for: Luxury party vacations, couples, group getaways

Budget Stay

Beach Huts (Palolem & Agonda)

Simple, charming beach huts right on the sand. Wake up to the sound of waves, step out onto the beach. Basic but unforgettable. The real Goa experience.

₹800 – ₹3,000/night

Best for: Solo travelers, backpackers, minimalist beach lovers

Gokarna

Luxury Stay

Swaswara Resort (Om Beach)

Wellness resort with private villas, yoga sessions, organic food, and panoramic sea views. A serene luxury escape on Gokarna's most famous beach.

₹15,000 – ₹30,000/night

Best for: Wellness retreats, couples seeking peace, digital detox

Budget Stay

Namaste Café & Guest House

Iconic Gokarna budget stay right at Om Beach. Basic rooms, great food, and the best location in town. The place where every Gokarna backpacker starts their trip.

₹800 – ₹2,500/night

Best for: Backpackers, solo travelers, beach camping vibe

Munnar

Luxury Stay

The Leaf Munnar Resort

Perched on a hilltop with panoramic views of the Western Ghats. Premium cottages, infinity pool, spa, and private tea garden walks. Munnar's finest.

₹10,000 – ₹22,000/night

Best for: Luxury hill station experience, honeymoon, family trips

Budget Stay

Tea County Homestays

Cozy rooms inside working tea estates. Wake up to mist rolling over the hills, home-cooked Kerala meals, and warm hosts who know every hidden trail.

₹1,200 – ₹3,500/night

Best for: Budget couples, nature lovers, peace seekers

DAY 1 The Epic Drive — BLR → Goa

5:00 AM departure from Bangalore. Take NH4 till Hubli → NH66 Karwar → Goa. Breakfast at Davanagere (Lincoln Restaurant). Lunch at Karwar beach shacks. Enter North Goa by 3:00 PM. Check in, unwind at Baga/Calangute sunset. Dinner at a beach shack with live music.

DAY 2 North Goa Beaches & Forts

Morning: Aguada Fort & Sinquerim Beach. Afternoon: Water sports at Baga. Evening: Anjuna Flea Market (Wednesdays) or Curlies Beach Shack at Anjuna. Night: Tito's Lane or Café Mambo for the party crowd.

DAY 3 Old Goa Heritage & Panjim

Morning: Basilica of Bom Jesus & Se Cathedral (UNESCO sites). Lunch at Panjim's Fontainhas Latin Quarter. Afternoon: Casino boat on Mandovi River or walk through colourful Portuguese streets. Evening: Dinner at Ritz Classic (Goan fish curry rice).

DAY 4 South Goa — Peace & Pristine Beaches

Drive south to Palolem & Agonda beaches. Kayaking through mangroves at Palolem. Relax on the quieter beaches. Butterfly Beach hike (hidden gem). Dinner at a beach bonfire. South Goa is the calm, beautiful side most tourists miss.

DAY 5 Return Drive to Bangalore

Early morning start (6:00 AM). Take the Anmod Ghat route via Dharwad for scenic mountain roads. Breakfast at Dharwad pedha shops. Lunch at Chitradurga Fort. Reach Bangalore by 7:00 PM. Trip of a lifetime. ✅

DAY 1 BLR → Munnar (via Coimbatore)

4:00 AM start. Drive via Salem → Coimbatore → Pollachi → Chinnar → Munnar. Breakfast at Salem A2B. Lunch at Coimbatore. Enter Kerala through the stunning Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary. Stop at Lakkam Waterfalls. Reach Munnar by 4 PM. Check into tea estate stay. Evening: Walk through the town and local market.

DAY 2 Kolukkumalai Sunrise + Tea Museum

3:30 AM departure for Kolukkumalai (world's highest tea plantation). Off-road jeep ride to the summit for the most epic sunrise in South India. (Featured in our video!). Return by 9 AM. Rest. 11 AM: Kannan Devan Tea Museum tour. 2 PM: Lunch. 4 PM: Echo Point & Mattupetty Dam. 6 PM: Speed boating at Kundala Lake.

DAY 3 Top Station + Eravikulam National Park

Morning: Eravikulam National Park (spot the endangered Nilgiri Tahr). Afternoon: Drive to Top Station (highest point on Munnar-Kodaikanal road, panoramic views of Tamil Nadu). Stop at Pothamedu View Point. Evening: Spice plantation visit and Ayurvedic massage. Dinner at Rapsy Restaurant (local favorite).

DAY 4 Return to BLR

Leisurely morning. Breakfast with mountain views. 9:30 AM: Start return journey via the scenic Munnar-Theni route (different from entry). Stop at Anayirankal Dam. Lunch at Dindigul (famous biryani!). Reach Bangalore by 8 PM with enough memories for a lifetime.
