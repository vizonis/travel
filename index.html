Complete Guide: Places to Visit Near Bangalore

Bangalore (Bengaluru), known as the Silicon Valley of India, is blessed with a geographic advantage that makes it one of the best cities for road trips and bike rides in South India. Surrounded by the Western Ghats, the Deccan Plateau, and the Malabar Coast, there are over 100 places to visit near Bangalore within a 500 km radius.

One Day Trips from Bangalore (Within 100 km)

If you're looking for a quick escape, there are excellent one day trips from Bangalore that you can complete on a bike or car. Nandi Hills (60 km) is the most famous sunrise destination, while Skandagiri (62 km) offers a thrilling night trek. Savandurga (60 km) is Asia's largest monolith hill, perfect for adventure seekers. Shivanasamudra Falls (135 km) features one of India's widest waterfalls, and Mekedatu (95 km) offers a beautiful river gorge experience.

Weekend Getaways from Bangalore (100-300 km)

For weekend trips, Coorg (250 km), Ooty (270 km), Chikmagalur (245 km), and Wayanad (280 km) are the top choices. These destinations offer a perfect mix of scenic drives, adventure activities, and relaxation. The road trip from Bangalore to Ooty via the Kalhatti Ghat with its 36 hairpin bends is particularly popular among biking enthusiasts.

Long Weekend Trips from Bangalore (300-500 km)

For longer getaways, Hampi (340 km) offers a surreal boulder-strewn landscape with ancient ruins, Gokarna (480 km) provides pristine beaches and coastal beauty, and Dandeli (460 km) is a hub for river rafting and jungle adventures. These adventure road trips from Bangalore are best explored over a 3-4 day long weekend.

Best Highways for Bike Rides from Bangalore

The NH44 (Bellary Road) towards Nandi Hills offers smooth six-lane riding. NH75 (Hassan Highway) is the gateway to Chikmagalur and Coorg with excellent road conditions. NH275 (Mysore Highway) features India's first elevated expressway and connects you to Mysore, Ooty, and Wayanad routes. NH48 (Mumbai Highway) takes you towards Gokarna and the Maharashtra coast.

Tips for Road Trips from Bangalore

  • Start before 5:30 AM to beat Bangalore's notorious traffic on all exit routes
  • Carry physical maps as backup — mobile network drops on ghat sections
  • Fuel up at highway fuel stations with clean restrooms (HPCL, IOCL)
  • Avoid night riding on ghat roads — visibility is poor and animals may cross
  • Check tire pressure and brake condition before every long ride
  • Keep emergency contacts saved — highway patrol: 1033, ambulance: 108
