Complete Bangalore Travel Guide

Bangalore, officially known as Bengaluru, is not just India's Silicon Valley — it's the gateway to some of South India's most spectacular travel destinations. Strategically located at the crossroads of Karnataka, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu, Bangalore serves as the perfect starting point for road trips to Coorg, Ooty, Wayanad, Goa, and Hampi.

The Bangalore to Coorg road trip is one of the most popular weekend drives, taking you through the scenic Mysore Highway and into the heart of Karnataka's coffee country. For beach lovers, the Bangalore to Goa road trip offers an unforgettable journey through the Western Ghats and along the Arabian Sea coast. History enthusiasts will love the Bangalore to Hampi drive, which transports you to the ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Whether you're searching for the best sunrise points near Bangalore (Nandi Hills tops the list), the most scenic road trips from Bangalore, or hidden weekend getaways that most tourists miss — our drone videos give you an aerial perspective that helps you plan the perfect trip. Each video includes GPS coordinates, route details, best time to visit, and insider tips.

FAQ

Travel Questions

Most asked questions about road trips and travel from Bangalore, answered with insights from our drone travel videos.

What are the best road trips from Bangalore?

Top road trips from Bangalore include Bangalore to Coorg (240 km), Bangalore to Chikkamagaluru (240 km), Bangalore to Ooty (270 km), Bangalore to Goa (560 km), Bangalore to Hampi (340 km), and Bangalore to Wayanad (280 km). Each route offers unique landscapes — from coffee plantations and mountain ghats to coastal highways and ancient ruins. Watch detailed road trip videos on the God of Drone YouTube channel for route maps and travel tips.

Which are the best weekend getaways from Bangalore?

Best weekend getaways from Bangalore include Nandi Hills (60 km), Coorg (240 km), Chikkamagaluru (240 km), Mysore (150 km), Wayanad (280 km), Yelagiri (170 km), Hogenakkal Falls (130 km), and Shivanasamudra Falls (135 km). God of Drone features stunning aerial videos of all these destinations, helping you choose your perfect weekend escape.

Where can I find drone videos of Bangalore tourist places?

God of Drone YouTube channel (@godofdrone) features stunning 4K drone videos of Bangalore tourist places, Karnataka temples, hill stations, waterfalls, and road trip routes with cinematic aerial footage. Subscribe for weekly uploads of the most scenic destinations in and around Bangalore.

What is the best time for road trips from Bangalore?

The best time for road trips from Bangalore is October to March when the weather is pleasant and roads are in good condition. Monsoon season (June-September) is ideal for waterfalls and lush green landscapes, though some ghat roads may be slippery. Summer trips to hill stations like Coorg, Chikkamagaluru, and Ooty are also popular as they offer cooler temperatures.

How far is Coorg from Bangalore and what's the best route?

Coorg is approximately 240 km from Bangalore, and the drive takes about 5-6 hours. The most popular route is Bangalore → Mysore → Madikeri via NH275. An alternative route goes through Channarayapatna and Kushalnagar. Watch our Bangalore to Coorg drone video for a complete aerial preview of the route with stop recommendations and scenic viewpoints.

Is drone flying allowed at tourist places near Bangalore?

Drone flying in India requires DGCA registration and a valid license under the Drone Rules 2021. Many tourist spots near Bangalore permit drone flying with proper permissions, while some areas near military installations and airports are restricted. God of Drone operates with all required DGCA certifications and permissions. Always check local regulations before flying drones at any destination.
